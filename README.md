# 📊 Matplotlib Sales Data Analysis

A beginner-friendly Data Visualization project built using **Python, Pandas, and Matplotlib**.

This project analyzes monthly **Sales and Profit** data and presents the results using different visualization techniques such as Line Chart, Bar Chart, Scatter Plot, and Histogram.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to analyze sales and profit data and represent important information visually.

The project demonstrates how Matplotlib can be used to:

- Analyze sales trends
- Compare monthly sales
- Understand the relationship between sales and profit
- Understand data distribution
- Identify sales performance against a target

---

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- 🐍 Python
- 📊 Pandas
- 📈 Matplotlib
- 💻 VS Code / Jupyter Notebook

---

## 📁 Dataset

The project uses a simple sample dataset containing:

| Month | Sales | Profit |
|------|------:|-------:|
| Jan | 100 | 20 |
| Feb | 130 | 30 |
| Mar | 120 | 25 |
| Apr | 160 | 40 |
| May | 180 | 50 |
| Jun | 200 | 60 |

---

## 📊 Visualizations Created

### 1. 📈 Monthly Sales Trend

A Line Chart is used to show how sales change from month to month.

**Purpose:**
- Identify sales trends
- Understand growth or decline over time

---

### 2. 📊 Monthly Sales Comparison

A Bar Chart is used to compare sales across different months.

**Purpose:**
- Compare individual months
- Identify the month with the highest sales

---

### 3. 🔵 Sales vs Profit

A Scatter Plot is used to visualize the relationship between Sales and Profit.

**Purpose:**
- Understand the relationship between two numerical variables
- Observe whether higher sales are associated with higher profit

---

### 4. 📉 Profit Distribution

A Histogram is used to understand the distribution of profit values.

**Purpose:**
- Understand the frequency of numerical values
- Analyze the distribution of profit

---

### 5. 🎯 Sales vs Target

A horizontal reference line is added at a sales target of **150**.

**Purpose:**
- Compare actual sales with the target
- Identify months performing above or below the target

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

From the analysis:

- 📈 Sales generally increased from January to June.
- 🏆 June recorded the highest sales of **200**.
- 📉 January recorded the lowest sales of **100**.
- 🎯 April, May, and June achieved sales above the target of **150**.
- 💰 Profit generally increased as sales increased in this sample dataset.
